# Food Ordering Customer App


## Key Features

### 🍔 Restaurant & Food Discovery
- Browse 100+ restaurants with filters
- High-resolution food images & descriptions
- Dietary tags (Vegan/Gluten-free/etc.)

### 🛒 Order Management
- Add items to cart from multiple restaurants
- Meal customization options
- Scheduled deliveries

### 💳 Secure Payments
- Chapa

### 🚚 Real-Time Tracking
- Live driver GPS tracking
- Cooking → Dispatched → Delivery timeline
- Delivery ETA countdown

### ⭐ Reviews & Ratings
- Rate restaurants, food items, and drivers
- Photo reviews
- Saved favorite orders
